About

I am a mechanical and aerospace engineer based in Casablanca, Morocco, currently working at Safran Engineering Services on LEAP 1A/1B engine components. My core expertise lies in non-conformance evaluation — performing integration and mechanical studies to determine whether deviated parts can be accepted for assembly, repaired, or rejected.

My daily work involves dimensional chain analysis (chaînes de cotes), functional dimensioning (cotation fonctionnelle), KT and inertia calculations, souplesse assessments, and structural simulation using CATIA V5 and PATRAN. Every disposition I issue carries direct airworthiness implications — a standard that has shaped my precision and judgment.

Beyond aerospace engineering, I have built a parallel career in AI model training and evaluation. I have trained and reviewed large language models for Outlier AI, and trained computer vision models for ABAKA AI / Remoexperts — bridging the gap between deep domain expertise and the frontier of artificial intelligence.

I hold a Master's in Aerospace Engineering from the International University of Rabat, and I operate fluently in Arabic, French (DALF C1), and English.
